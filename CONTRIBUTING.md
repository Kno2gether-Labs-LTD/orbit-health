# Contributing to Orbit Health

Thank you for wanting to help!

**Pull requests aren't being accepted yet.** Orbit Health is still being set up, and there isn't enough maintainer time to review contributions properly. Contributions will open soon; this page will say so when they do.

## What helps right now
- **Ring reports.** Open an issue with the *Feedback / ring report* template: which ring, which phone and Android version, what worked and what didn't.
- **Unsupported rings.** Tell us the ring's name, where you bought it and its vendor app. [docs/adding-a-ring.md](docs/adding-a-ring.md) explains how a new ring family is added.
- **Ideas.** Tell us what would make Orbit Health your daily health app.

Never post exports of your own health data, or Bluetooth captures from your ring, in a public issue. They identify you.

## Security problems
Please don't open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
